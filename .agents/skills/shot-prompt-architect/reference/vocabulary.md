# Vocabulary: the dials and what each one does

A prompt is a set of dials, not a spell. You should be able to point at any word in it and
say which dial it turns: shot size, angle, lens, lighting, movement, capture, grade. This
file is the "what it does to the viewer" column, in our words, from AiKAMI Days 2 and 3 and
PJ's lens table. Use the effect column to choose; use the term column to write. Never a
person's name; the look is described, not attributed.

## Shot size (distance)

| Term | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| extreme wide shot | establishes place and time; the opening of a film or scene |
| wide shot | establishes a location; the relationship between a subject and their environment |
| full shot | introduces a character in a setting; body language and movement read |
| medium shot | dialogue distance; comfortable for two or more people talking |
| medium close-up | chest up; intimacy while the environment still reads |
| close-up | emotion; a detail the viewer must notice |
| extreme close-up, macro | tension or intimacy; a small detail made the whole subject (eyes, a fastener, a texture) |

## Camera angle

| Term | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| eye-level | neutral, direct, equal; the viewer is in the room |
| high angle | the subject smaller, vulnerable, overpowered |
| low angle | the subject powerful, dominant, heroic |
| bird's-eye, god's-eye, top-down | overview of a scene; relationships and scale; a figure as a dot in a grid |
| worm's-eye | larger than life; the viewer small |
| dutch tilt | unease, disorientation, chaos |
| over-the-shoulder | point of view between two characters; confrontation or intimacy in dialogue |
| POV | what a character sees; immersion, a specific emotional experience |
| three-quarter, low front three-quarter hero angle | the front and side of a subject in one view; vehicles and portraits read fullest |

## Field of view and lens, by outcome

| Term | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 14mm ultra-wide, 107° | large-scale geography, dramatic sweep, strong edge distortion, FPV feel |
| 24mm wide, 84° | expansive environment with a close intimate face still readable; subtle edge distortion |
| 35mm, 47° | natural everyday framing, documentary action, standard normal |
| 50mm | human-eye perspective, neutral |
| 85mm portrait, 29° | compressed background, creamy shallow bokeh, medium portrait |
| 135mm telephoto, 18° | heavy compression, isolated subject, flat depth, tight emotional close-up |
| 300mm and beyond, 8° | distant hidden observation, super-telephoto |
| macro | pores, texture, product detail magnified |
| anamorphic | 2.39:1 scope, oval bokeh, horizontal flares, cinematic width |
| vintage spherical prime | soft imperfect glass, organic filmic character |
| warm painterly prime ("the flattering look") | soft skin, warm rendering |
| clinical modern prime | clinically sharp, clean precision |
| swirly-bokeh vintage lens | dreamy vignette edges, swirling out-of-focus background |
| shallow depth of field | subject sharp, background melted |
| deep focus | whole frame crisp front to back; formal, controlled |
| lens flare, bokeh | streaks and glints when light hits the glass; dreaminess, isolation |

Lock one lens character across the cuts of a shot; change it only when the content class
changes, with a hard cut, never a drift.

## Lighting

| Term | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| high key | background and subject evenly lit, low contrast; happier scenes |
| low key | uneven, high contrast; darker, dramatic scenes |
| chiaroscuro | low key at a high contrast ratio; mysterious, dangerous |
| hard light | directional, sharp-edged shadows; intense, dramatic |
| soft light | diffused, few shadows; romantic, gentle |
| volumetric, god rays | light travelling through dust, fog, haze, rain; memorable frames |
| bokeh light | out-of-focus light; isolation, dreaminess, curiosity |
| silhouette, backlit, rim light only | subject against a brighter background; mystery, concealed identity |
| butterfly / beauty light | frontal soft key from above; clean beauty portraits |
| harsh midday sun, no fill | hard-edged short shadows, blown specular highlights on skin and sweat |
| golden hour, backlit | warm, lifted, halation, hair and breath in the light |
| sodium and cyan night | neon noir; crushed shadows with colour in them |
| fluorescent institutional overheads | cold green tint, sickly palette, clinical stillness |

Lighting is a constraint, not decoration: state direction and quality, and protect it
("faces fall into shadow unless explicitly lit, no flat frontal key, no beauty fill").

## Camera movement

| Term | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| locked off, static | formality, stillness, a held composition |
| slow push-in | attention narrowing, dread or intimacy building |
| dolly back, pull back with an arc | reveal; the subject placed in the world |
| lateral truck, tracking alongside | speed and continuity; the environment streaks past |
| tracking backward ahead of the subject | the subject drives at the lens; explosive forward drive |
| crane / jib up | scale, majesty, an ending |
| descending push from above | descent onto a subject; surveillance, pressure |
| orbit / arc | the subject as a sculpture; time to look |
| whip pan, whip zoom, crash zoom | violent energy, a snap of attention, cuts on the beat |
| handheld following | breathing frame, documentary intimacy |
| steadicam glide | continuous, choreographed, unbroken |
| FPV flown | micro-corrections, breathing distance, uneven roll rate, prop-wash wobble |
| rack focus | a shift of attention from one plane to another inside a beat |
| slow motion, speed ramp | a moment designed, not a setting: full speed, hard drop at the impact beat, ramp back |

## Cut types

HARD CUT (default), SMASH CUT (abrupt contrast), MATCH CUT (shape or motion carries across),
INSERT CUT (a detail that needs its own frame), REVERSE CUT (the other side of the axis),
WHIP CUT (a whip pan hides the cut). No fades, crossfades or dissolves unless asked. Every
cut preserves characters, geography, screen direction, gaze, lighting direction, wardrobe
and prop state.

## Speed words

"normal speed", "fast speed", "extreme slow motion, roughly 10% speed", "speed ramp down at
the 3-second mark", "speed ramps back up to normal at the 7-second mark", "real time, no
cuts". Say the ramp on both sides. Slow motion must be crisp; exclude motion blur inside it.

## Style, not names

The guides attribute looks to directors and cinematographers. We do not; the preset carries
the look. The translations the repo uses:

| Look the guides mean | Our words |
| --- | --- |
| vast, minimal, awe-inducing sci-fi grandeur | extreme wide, tiny figure in negative space, diffused sunless glow, monumental scale; `anamorphic-epic` |
| master natural light | motivated single source, soft wrap, protected shadows |
| cold, precise, dark, meticulously controlled | locked-off symmetrical frame, cold green-tinted overheads, clinical stillness, fine skin texture |
| symmetrical pastel whimsy, flat framing | `symmetry-pastel` |
| grand IMAX-scale practical realism | large-format film texture, practical effects, deep focus |
| dreamy golden hour, poetic natural light | `golden-hour-romance` |
| saturated neon, moody, romantic blur | `neon-noir`, step-printed motion blur, saturated signage |
| one-point perspective, cold precision | centred symmetric wide, deep focus, slow zoom |
| bold, punchy, retro-cool framing | low angles, hard cuts, saturated primaries, trunk shots |
| warm, tactile, character-driven soft naturalism | soft daylight, film grain, muted warm palette |
| long immersive takes, floating camera | `one-take` |
| sweeping natural-light wide-angle, immersive flow | wide lens handheld, continuous move, available light |
| high-contrast desaturated epic slow motion | `impact-slow-motion` |
| soft, dreamy, pastel, quietly melancholic | pastel palette, soft light, negative space, held frames |
| neon-drenched, hypnotic, bold saturated colour | saturated single-colour washes, slow rotation, strobe |
