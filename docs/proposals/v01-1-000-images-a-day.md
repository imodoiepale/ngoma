## First 30 days

1. **Week 1: prove it.** Run the template on the pod with owned or fictional inputs; record real GPU minutes and replace the 55 GPU-hour estimate.
2. **Week 2: pilot.** One client at a reduced price in exchange for a case study and permission to show results.
3. **Weeks 3-4: sell.** Pitch social media agencies managing 10+ accounts with the pilot; target the low scenario (1 clients) before scaling.
4. **Automate** the recurring delivery with the studio canvas and the dry-run plan check.
